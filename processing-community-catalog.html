Processing Community Catalog

A 2021 catalogue celebrating 20 years of the Processing community.

This was a catalogue dedicated to celebrating 20 years of Processing in 2021, where members of the Processing community were invited to share their contributions as well as anything made with anything related to (and including) Processing. At the time, I was learning about creative coding through Processing and I also released my first actual portfolio website.

To see my contribution to the catalogue directly, GOTO CON 003 - Page 318 within the 20th Anniversary Processing Community Catalog.

You can also view the entire catalogue online via the Internet Archive.
