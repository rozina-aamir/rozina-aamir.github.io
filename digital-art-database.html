Digital Art Database

The Digital Art Database curates things that exist at the intersection of art and technology in an A-Z list, inspired by retro aesthetics and the command line interface.

Originally created on 13th July 2022 in the spirit of highly influential projects along the likes of Wikipedia and the Internet Archive, the Digital Art Database is a website that collects and curates different things that exist at the intersection of art and technology by listing and linking a range of different artists, companies, collectives, and others all in one place.
