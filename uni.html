University Research Blog

This was a private blog (hosted on Tumblr between October 2022 to July 2025) where I predominantly documented my research and development process throughout my final year of University, as well as the first two years of post-grad life.

Although the blog did exist on Tumblr, archived versions of the blog are very hard to find, as it was only made public for an extremely short amount of time.

Themes within the blog involved during my final year of University involved researching the intersection of art and technology, the attention economy, and sensory overload.

As a result, my dissertation focused on how sculptures existed in physical and digital contexts, and my final major project focused on creating readymade sculptures using physical pieces of obsolete technology to reflect the attention economy.

After graduating, I documented different aspects of my life at the time (usually in the form of long form essays mixed with stream-of-consciousness journaling, as well as a bit more informal research into the topics I explored at University), in which I mainly thought about my career, and my decision to redesign and commercialise my Digital Art Database project, which originally started out as a way to gather interesting research material into one place, and something that I once considered evolving into a full-fledged media empire, before realising that commercialising the Digital Art Database wasn't the right thing to do.

I eventually ended the blog as it eventually prevented me from taking action on the things that I planned to do, and also because I realised that outstretching a University module 2 years past its due date would no longer be a viable or sustainable option for me.
