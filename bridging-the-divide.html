Bridging The Divide

A product and service design proposal to prevent the spread of fake news, negativity, and desensitisation by encouraging and promoting positivity as well as good mental health habits and mindfulness for users on Twitter.

This was originally designed in response to an open call for the 2021 RSA Student Design Awards, where the main theme of the open call was to promote mindful online habits as well as reducing polarisation and fostering connection on social media platforms.

The original user interfaces that I designed for this open call were static, imagined screenshots of how those features would have been implemented on social media platforms such as Twitter.
